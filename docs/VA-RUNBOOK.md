# VA runbook for the Hadaway Ct owner-finance campaign

You are promoting one property, 1244 Hadaway Ct NE, Lawrenceville GA 30043, through owner financing. Everything you post must come from data/ad-copy.json in this repo. Do not invent terms, prices, or dates. If a buyer asks something not covered there, log the question and say the owner will confirm.

## The terms, memorize these

Price $580,000. Down payment from $35,000. About $4,190 per month, 8.5% fixed, 30-year schedule, 5-year balloon. Close in 7 to 14 days. GA attorney closes, buyer gets the warranty deed on day one, owner holds a security deed, third-party loan servicer from day one.

Never offer lower down payment terms. The $35K floor is deliberate. A buyer with $20K only moves forward if they pass full income documentation plus a note-buyer pre-commitment, and the owner decides that, not you.

## Daily routine, in order

1. FB Marketplace. Repost or bump the listing every morning before 10am ET. Use the facebook_marketplace title and body from ad-copy.json. Replace [YOUR PHONE] with the campaign phone number.
2. Craigslist. Repost daily in 2 metro areas, Atlanta and Athens. Rotate between the two craigslist_titles so the same title never posts two days in a row. Flag and repost if ghosted within 4 hours.
3. FB Groups. Post in 3 different local groups per day. Never the same group twice within 24 hours. Use facebook_groups_short. Target groups, Gwinnett County buy/sell, Lawrenceville community groups, metro Atlanta rent-to-own and owner finance groups.
4. BiggerPockets. Post the biggerpockets_marketplace text once per week in the marketplace area. Reply to any DM within 1 hour during your shift.
5. Note buyers. Email a batch of 10 per day from data/outreach-targets.json using note_buyer_email, until the list is exhausted. Personalize the first line with the company name. Track every send.
6. Log every lead, inquiry, and send in data/leads.csv the moment it happens. Include channel, date, buyer name, contact, and status.

## Buyer screening, before you share anything detailed

Every buyer gets screened before you send full terms, photos of the interior, or a showing slot. Collect, credit report or authorization to pull, proof of income (2 years tax returns if self-employed), and employment verification. Build the ability-to-repay file. Only after the file passes do you schedule a showing.

Buyers who push back on screening are not buyers. Log them as declined and move on.

## Objections

Use the objection_handling array in ad-copy.json word for word until you're comfortable, then keep the substance and vary the wording. The five covered are risk, monthly payment vs rent, price vs comps, refinance, and down payment size. Anything else gets logged and escalated.

## Hard rules

Never negotiate price or terms yourself. Route anything past "I'll negotiate on strong files" to the owner.
Never promise a specific close date. Say 7 to 14 days is typical.
Never represent the property as anything but As Is.
Never post the same ad text to the same platform twice in 24 hours, rotate titles and slight wording.
Never share the Zillow, Redfin, or MLS links with buyers before screening. Those reveal the 56 days on market and the price cut.
Post only during 8am to 9pm ET.

## Market context, for your own confidence

Gwinnett median value is about $403K as of August 2026 and down 2.5% year over year. Median days on market is around 35 to 50 depending on source. Competitors in the area advertise $20K to $40K down or 10% down. About half of area listings are not selling. A $35K down, 8.5% fixed offer with a 10-day close is competitive for this market, so don't apologize for the terms.

## Escalation

Anything involving contract negotiation, attorney questions, note-sale pricing, or media attention goes to the owner the same day. Everything else you handle.

Humanize gate passed, no hard tells.
